# Print & Bulletin Exports

While Cue Central champions digital, screen-based stage management, it provides robust print export engines for podium binders, traditional church bulletins, and sound booth clipboards.

---

## 🖨️ Printable Cue Sheets (`print.php`)

Directors can print clean physical cue sheets formatted specifically for 8.5" x 11" paper:
* **Podium Printout**: Large, legible print formatted for the pastor and song leader.
* **Sound Booth Sheet**: High-density grid highlighting microphone assignments, audio playback cues, and lighting triggers.
* **Accompanist Order**: Condensed list showing hymn numbers, key signatures, and modulation notes.

---

## 📋 Schedule Matrix (`print_schedule.php`)

Generate full-page monthly schedule grids suitable for physical bulletin boards or choir room display cases:
* Lists all service dates, choir openers, soloists, accompanists, and offertories.
* Compact layout fits an entire month on a single sheet of paper.
