# Congregational Hymns & Preludes

In addition to choral repertoire, Cue Central manages church-wide congregational hymnals and instrumental prelude rotations.

---

## 📖 The Hymn Index (`hymns`)

Cue Central stores a full catalog of congregational hymns:
* **Hymn Number**: Number in the physical pew hymnal.
* **Hymn Name**: Primary title (e.g. *Holy, Holy, Holy*, *A Mighty Fortress Is Our God*).
* **Key & Meter**: Musical key signature and poetic meter.
* **Author & Tune Name**: Historical hymnology details.

### Monthly Chorus Hymn
Directors can designate a **Hymn of the Month** in `monthly_settings.chorus_hymn_id`. This hymn is highlighted on dashboard banners and scheduled throughout the month to teach new congregational songs to the church family.

---

## 🎹 Prelude Sets (`prelude_sets` & `prelude_items`)

Prelude music sets the spiritual atmosphere as the congregation enters the auditorium:

* **Prelude Sets**: Pre-arranged thematic medleys (e.g. *Grace & Mercy Set*, *Thanksgiving Medley*, *Communion Instrumental*).
* **Pianist Rotation**: Assigned accompanists can review their required pieces days in advance.
* **Duration Calculations**: Estimated runtime calculations ensure preludes conclude precisely as the pastoral staff takes the platform.
